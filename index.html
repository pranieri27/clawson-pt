About Us

Meet Our Team

David T. Clawson, M.P.T.

Physical Therapist — David earned his Bachelor's degree in Pre-Physical Therapy from California Lutheran University in 1988 and his Master's degree in Physical Therapy from Chapman University/Children's Hospital of Los Angeles in 1990.

He has practiced in the Sacramento region for over 30 years, including seven years in skilled nursing care as a Director of Rehabilitation. He has taught at both American River and Sacramento City Colleges, and participated in establishing Sutter General's Transitional Care Center.

Debra Lamb, PTA

Physical Therapist Assistant — Debra graduated from Sacramento City College in 1999 earning her Associate's Degree. She has worked in acute care as well as outpatient facilities in both California and Washington for over 20 years.

Our Services

Comprehensive Physical Therapy

We treat a variety of diagnoses with personalized care plans tailored to your unique needs and recovery goals.

Orthopedic Therapy

Treatment for neck, back, shoulder pain, total joint replacements, and work-related orthopedic injuries with targeted rehabilitation programs.

Neurological Rehab

Specialized therapy for stroke recovery, neurological impairments, and conditions affecting movement, balance, and coordination.

Pediatric Therapy

Working with children as young as six months to treat developmental delays, balance deficits, walking difficulties, and tone reduction.

Post-Surgical Care

Comprehensive rehabilitation following joint replacements, arthroscopic procedures, and other surgical interventions to restore function.

Work Injury Recovery

Return-to-work programs designed to help you safely resume occupational activities after workplace injuries.

Balance & Mobility

Fall prevention programs, balance training, gait assessment, and mobility enhancement for improved safety and independence.
